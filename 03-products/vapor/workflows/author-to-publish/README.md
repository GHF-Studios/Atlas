# Author to Publish

> **Status:** Draft — authoring and review surfaces exist; server-owned publishing is incomplete.

Describe how a creator authors a content project, validates its role and metadata, previews dependencies, stages a review, and explicitly confirms publication. Studio supports local discovery, manifest editing, and read-only composition preview; publishing authority and Registry catalog work remain separate.

Do not equate saving metadata, creating a project, or staging a review with publishing.
