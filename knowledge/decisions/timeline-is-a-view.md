Atlas will not organize every record by date. Questions, decisions, evidence, and work have their own relationships and may have no meaningful date. Dated events can be rendered as a timeline without forcing every record into chronology.

The timeline should be a prominent way into Atlas, but it is one view over the wider knowledge structure rather than the underlying data model.
