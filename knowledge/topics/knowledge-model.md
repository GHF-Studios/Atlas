The primary structure is a navigable graph of typed records. Questions, decisions, evidence, topics, events, and workstreams can refer to one another. Parentage gives a readable hierarchy; typed links express other relationships.

Dates are optional attributes. A timeline is generated from dated event records, while undated questions, decisions, and evidence remain discoverable in the map. The knowledge model is broader than its timeline view.

This is the agreed direction for the bootstrap. Individual record fields and relationship semantics remain open for review.
