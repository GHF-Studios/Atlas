Atlas is GHF Studios' canonical, versioned place for understanding how its products, plans, decisions, evidence, and history fit together.

The repository is the durable source. Websites and other views are generated from it. Atlas should help a person find their bearings and decide what to read or do next, rather than present a pile of unrelated documents.

This first review slice explains Atlas itself and preserves the earlier Loo-Cast/Vapor audit. It does not treat that audit as settled truth or import the wider ecosystem as canon.
