Atlas is a GHF Studios product: a canonical, versioned place for understanding how the organization's products, plans, decisions, evidence, and history fit together.

The repository is the durable source. Websites and other views are generated from it. Atlas should help a person find their bearings and decide what to read or do next, rather than present a pile of unrelated documents.

Atlas is the subject of this first knowledge set. The earlier Loo-Cast/Vapor audit is preserved as historical working evidence, not adopted as settled truth or imported as canon.
