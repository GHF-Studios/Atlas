## Product identity

The owner identifies Vapor, Loo-Cast, and Atlas as three distinct GHF Studios products. Atlas is the highest-level product in the current product picture. That is an explicit product direction, not a claim inferred from repository structure.

Atlas exists to help people understand the products, their history, decisions, evidence, open questions, and future direction. Atlas is the product being built here; references to Vapor and Loo-Cast provide organizational context only. This bootstrap does not investigate or integrate either product.

## Product boundary

The Atlas Git repository is the durable authored source. The published website is one user-facing way to browse it. The generated JSON is a machine-facing read view. Neither output replaces the records or owns a separate copy of their meaning.

Atlas is intended to become a dependable way for its owner and collaborators to orient themselves, inspect how claims are supported, and find the next relevant question or action. The exact product scope and workflows remain under active design.

## Evidence boundary

The owner supplied the product relationship and intent in conversation. The current implementation is independently inspectable in this repository; follow the Atlas system records linked from the homepage. Do not read “highest-level” as a settled corporate or technical hierarchy beyond that direction.
