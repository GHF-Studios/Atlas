# Foundations

This section records why GHF Studios exists, what it seeks to build, and which values and principles should guide its choices. It is the starting point for the studio’s vision, mission, philosophy, strategy, and non-goals.

The topics move from identity and purpose through present work, future direction, beliefs, guiding ideas, and goals, then boundaries and open questions. Purpose and mission, vision and ambitions, and values, convictions, and principles overlap; keeping them separate here leaves those distinctions open for review rather than implying they are settled.

## Current state — 2026-11-13

The underlying ideas have developed over years, but they are scattered across conversations, prototypes, and technical decisions. We do **not** yet have a complete, carefully human-reviewed foundation. What appears here begins as a draft, not automatically as established doctrine.
