# Foundations

This section records why GHF Studios exists, what it seeks to build, and which values and principles should guide its choices.
It is the starting point for our vision, mission, philosophy, strategy, and non-goals.

## Current state — 2026-11-11

The underlying ideas have developed over years, but they are scattered across conversations, prototypes, and technical decisions.
We do **not** yet have a complete, carefully human-reviewed foundation. What appears here begins as a draft, not automatically as established doctrine.
