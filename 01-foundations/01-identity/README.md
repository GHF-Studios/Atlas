# Identity

- **Who we are:** We use GHF Studios as the umbrella for Vapor, the Spacetime Engine, Loo Cast, and our broader ambitions ([Atlas overview](../../README.md)). Our shared development policy applies across GHF Studios projects ([policy](https://github.com/GHF-Studios/Vapor-Client/blob/2c7e534/.agents/active-development-policy.md)). This records our current name and scope, not a legal status or formal organization.
- **What we believe:** Some assumptions about what is possible are simply wrong. We are not here to prove them wrong; we are here to build what we already believe is possible.
- **Vapor's role:** Vapor is the glue—the mortar between the bricks—built for an assumption-breaking way to create engines, games, and possibly software more broadly. It adapts to what we need now and in the foreseeable future, and carries the work meant to keep improving over time.
- **What we are building toward:** We aim to create the Spacetime Engine and fully specify and implement its contract. It is a more concrete concept than Vapor; Loo Cast is less concrete than the Engine, but still more concrete than Vapor, and shows how the Engine can be used.
- **Why this is personal:** This work is personal and financially significant to me. I turn anger, frustration, and anxiety into fuel for what we are building; sometimes it feels like others would rather not see what we see.... Fuck the haters!
- **Boundaries:** This entry is about the identity and philosophy that tie the studio's work together. Product descriptions belong in the [Studio Portfolio](../../03-products/README.md); community channels belong in [Community](../../02-ecosystem/community/README.md); people, roles, legal identity, and responsibility belong in [Organization](../../06-organization/README.md). Purpose, mission, vision, and philosophy have their own entries in this section.
- **Still open:** This is only the beginning of our account of who we are and what drives this work. We still need to articulate the broader philosophy behind this relationship.
