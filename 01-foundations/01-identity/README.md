# Identity

- **Current studio-wide name:** Atlas presents GHF Studios as the umbrella for its work, including Atlas, Vapor, Spacetime Engine, Loo Cast, and broader ambitions ([Atlas overview](../../README.md)). The owner-provided development policy also applies across GHF Studios projects ([policy](https://github.com/GHF-Studios/Vapor-Client/blob/2c7e534/.agents/active-development-policy.md)). This establishes current usage, not legal status or formal organization.
- **Confirmed platform records:** The [Vapor Registry GitHub account record](https://github.com/GHF-Studios/Vapor-Registry/blob/3ecf486ca9c4dab19bce99c08ac24963c9d74bc0/registry/github/accounts/Leslieghf.toml) identifies the `GHF-Studios` organization. The [Steam app record](https://github.com/GHF-Studios/Vapor-Registry/blob/3ecf486ca9c4dab19bce99c08ac24963c9d74bc0/registry/steam/apps/2122620.toml) lists Loo-Cast under that organization. These confirm repository and product-distribution identities, not a standalone studio storefront or complete public profile.
- **Public presentation:** The [Vapor Homepage Server](https://github.com/GHF-Studios/Vapor-Platform-Server/blob/65ade3c28e66f299d7ecc303af54ab3391c63610/Vapor-Homepage-Server/README.md) is scoped to Vapor's product and legal/contact surfaces and explicitly excludes identity authority. A canonical studio-wide website, public description, Discord, and contact email have not been verified in the sources reviewed here.
- **Boundaries:** Product names and descriptions belong in the [Studio Portfolio](../../03-products/README.md); community participation and channels belong in [Community](../../02-ecosystem/community/README.md); people, roles, legal identity, and responsibility belong in [Organization](../../06-organization/README.md). Purpose and future ambition belong in their own Foundation entries.
- **Still open:** the approved public description of GHF Studios, its confirmed official public channels, and whether there is a durable studio-wide identity beyond the identities and visions of its projects.
