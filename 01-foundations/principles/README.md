# Guiding Principles

> **Status:** Draft — proposed guidance, not approved policy.

Principles are concise rules of thumb for making choices when goals, values, or constraints pull in different directions. Each should explain the situation it addresses and the trade-off it accepts.

Do not treat the child topics as agreed principles merely because folders exist. Keep them distinct from [values](../values/README.md), which describe what matters, and from operational or engineering standards.
