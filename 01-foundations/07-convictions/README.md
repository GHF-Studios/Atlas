# Convictions

- **Claim:** We know some assumptions about what is possible are utterly wrong.
- **Basis:** This is the conviction behind our stated [Purpose](../02-purpose/README.md); the specific assumptions and reasons are not yet written down.
- **Limit:** This does not mean every design idea is correct or already implemented; many remain open in [Vision](../04-vision/README.md).
- **Practical consequence:** We build from what we already believe possible; proving the assumptions wrong to others is not our purpose.
- **Still open:** Which assumptions do we mean, and what exactly makes us sure they are wrong?
