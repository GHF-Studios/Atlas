# Long-Term Ambitions

The Spacetime Engine and Loo Cast vision is our central long-range ambition; its shape belongs in [Vision](../04-vision/README.md). This page records only horizons beyond that work.

- **Possible wider horizon — not adopted:** Vapor's adaptable, contract-based approach might help us create software beyond engines and games. We have floated software in general as a possibility, but have not decided to pursue or defined that scope.
- **Historical context:** The [historical intent reconstruction](https://github.com/GHF-Studios/Atlas/blob/874a24a/archive/audit-snapshot-2026-10-26/loo-cast-vapor-origin-and-intent.md) gathers older plans and interpretations; treat it as context, not current commitments.
- **Still open:** Do we want Vapor to reach beyond the needs of our engine and games? Are there other long-term aims beyond the central vision, and who would they serve?
