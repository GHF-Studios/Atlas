# Start Here

> **Status:** Draft — intended for human review.

Atlas is GHF Studios' human-maintained knowledge base: its Markdown is human-authored or receives substantial human review. This folder is the **reading and writing guide** for Atlas: how to find information, capture ideas, distinguish evidence from intentions, and improve documents over time.

It is **not** the complete table of contents. That lives in the repository root: [Table of Contents](../TABLE_OF_CONTENTS.md).

## Your first three stops

1. Read the [root README](../README.md) to understand **why Atlas exists**.
2. Open the [Table of Contents](../TABLE_OF_CONTENTS.md) to see **what Atlas covers**.
3. Visit a section's `README.md` to understand **what belongs there and what is actually established**.

You do not need to read or complete everything. Most topic pages now contain short draft bullet notes for human review; treat them as research leads and tentative summaries, **not** approved policy or finished guidance. Some folders still hold structural placeholders.

## Working principle

**Write something imperfect, label it accurately, and improve it when you learn more.**

An empty file can be a useful reminder. A speculative paragraph can be useful research. Neither should be mistaken for an approved decision or verified implementation.

## Current reality

As of November 2026, Atlas is being designed rather than operated as a mature documentation program. These guides are proposals for human review, not evidence that their practices have been adopted. They do not establish heavyweight review or approval requirements for a pre-alpha, founder-led project. Their purpose is to make human authorship and review easier, not to make writing feel like paperwork.
